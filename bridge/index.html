Bridge · from device to camera

BovineLabs Bridge is the seam between an ECS entity and the GameObject world Cinemachine and the Input System still live in. These four maps follow one input in and one camera pose out. The load-bearing idea across all of them: Bridge is a sync layer, not a smoothing layer — it reproduces faithfully whatever discontinuity already exists.
